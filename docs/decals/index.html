Application

Print and fitting rules

  1. Convert to the printer's CMYK profile. The source artwork is RGB.
  2. Matte or satin vinyl. Gloss reflects badly under exhibition lighting.
  3. Laminate everything. The limbs strike the ground every gait cycle.
  4. Degrease with isopropyl alcohol. Never apply over fresh anodising or uncured powder coat.
  5. Keep decals 10 mm clear of panel edges and fasteners so they survive removal.
  6. Do not cover the E-stop mushroom head. The centre of that decal is a cut-out.
Not certified labels: the hazard pictograms follow ISO 7010 geometry so they read correctly, but they are original artwork and carry no certification. Source certified labels wherever compliance is actually required, and treat the pinch-point stripes and E-stop surround as safety equipment rather than styling.
