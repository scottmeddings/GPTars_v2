Power baseline

12.8 V LiFePO4 architecture

Selected battery

WattCycle 12.8 V 100 Ah Mini

  • Nominal energy1,280 Wh
  • Battery mass~9.5 kg
  • BMS class100 A
  • Continuous envelope~1.28 kW nominal
  • PlacementLow + central

Design decision

Keep the walking robot light

  • 100 Ah MiniSelected
  • 314 Ah packRejected for mobile build
  • Robot mass target20–23 kg
  • Review threshold25 kg
  • Redesign threshold30+ kg
Design rule: battery capacity is not free. Extra stored energy adds structural load, raises actuator torque requirements and can trigger a weight spiral. The 100 Ah Mini is the current compromise between runtime and mobility.

05

Power modes

Do not run everything flat-out all day

ModeBehaviour
AwakeFull conversation, vision, display and approved robot functions available.
StandbyMotors disabled, reduced camera rate, display dimmed and heavy AI services idle where possible; wake word remains active.
SleepOnly the safety/wake subsystem and minimum supervisory electronics remain active; high-power compute can be shut down.
Critical batteryMotion restricted, nonessential loads disabled and TARS transitions toward safe shutdown or charging.

Mass gate: around 25 kg we reassess actuator torque, structural mass and gait assumptions. At 30 kg or more, the design should be reviewed before simply fitting larger motors.
